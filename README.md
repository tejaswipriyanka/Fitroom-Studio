# FitRoom Studio 👗
### Your Personal Wardrobe & Styling Atelier

**FitRoom Studio** is a smart fashion and personal styling platform designed to help users explore their style, organize their wardrobe, and make better outfit choices. It aims to simplify everyday fashion decisions through a personalized digital styling experience.

## 🌐 Live Website
[Visit FitRoom Studio](https://fitroom-studio.ai.studio/)

## ✨ Project Overview
FitRoom Studio brings fashion inspiration and personal wardrobe styling into one digital experience. It focuses on helping users discover their fashion preferences and create looks that reflect their individual style.

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- Simplify everyday outfit selection.
- Encourage personalized fashion choices.
- Make wardrobe planning more convenient.
- Help users explore and express their personal style.
- Create a smarter and more engaging fashion experience.

## 💡 Vision
To transform everyday dressing into a simple, personalized, and enjoyable experience through technology and fashion.
